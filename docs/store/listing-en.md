## Full description (3409 of 4000)

<!--full-->
Every day you get a ticket. One number: what you can spend today, so your money reaches the end of the month.

Masroufi is not a bank and not a wallet. It is a ledger that does the maths for you and talks like a sensible friend from the neighbourhood.

How it works
• Morning: a fresh ticket with today's number.
• All day: log a spend in two taps. Each entry punches a hole in the ticket and the number drops in front of you. Repeated spends (transport, coffee) take one tap.
• Night: tear the ticket. What is left goes to the jar (or to tomorrow, or to the emergency envelope) and a folk proverb card flips into your album.
• Over the ticket? No blame. The extra is spread over the remaining days.

Free, and complete
• End-of-month mode: when money gets tight, it splits what is left into small daily tickets and reserves the bills. Free forever.
• The roll: every day is a stub on a roll, with search and filters.
• Pockets: cash, mobile wallet, bank and "under the tile" money, plus a cash counter. Paste a bank or wallet message and the app reads it on your phone and suggests an entry that you confirm.
• Envelopes: budgets drawn as paper envelopes, up to 4.
• The jar and goals: what is left of your tickets adds up, and you can aim at a goal such as a phone or a trip.
• People: loans, the grocer's tab, quick bill splitting, and a gentle nudge that never embarrasses anyone.
• Gameya: one savings circle, with whose turn it is and who has paid.
• Weekly match: this week against last week. A day under the ticket is a goal for you; a day over is a goal against.
• Before you buy: type what you want and its price, see what it costs in daily tickets, and park it for 48 hours.
• Subscriptions and installments: everything that renews by itself, every installment, and your freedom date.
• Pay yourself a salary if your income comes in lumps, and a kids' allowance with a child screen behind a PIN.
• The monthly story: five slides about your month, shareable without amounts.
• Widgets for the ticket and the match, and an evening reminder if you want one.

Your privacy
• No account and no server. Your money and your data stay on your phone.
• The app does not ask for the internet permission. No ads, no analytics.
• App lock with PIN or fingerprint, hide amounts, and hide the screen in recent apps.
• Backup to an encrypted file with a password you choose, saved wherever you choose. If you forget the password nobody can recover it. A weekly automatic backup is there if you turn it on.
• Arabic and English.

Masroufi Plus (optional)
Plus adds tools for planning ahead: the jar in gold and round-ups, seasons (Ramadan, Eid, school) with a zakat calculator, the duty ledger for wedding gifts, your basket and habit facts, installment-versus-cash comparison, unlimited envelopes, gameyas and children, the full match history, and a safety ticket for your streak.
• The price and the billing period are shown by Google Play for your country.
• The subscription renews automatically at the end of each period until you cancel it.
• Cancel any time in Google Play: Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions.
• The free version stays usable without paying, and nothing you entered is deleted if Plus stops.
• A 14-day trial of Plus starts from inside the app, with no card.

Masroufi only records. It never moves money and it is not financial advice. Bank and wallet names appear as plain text.
