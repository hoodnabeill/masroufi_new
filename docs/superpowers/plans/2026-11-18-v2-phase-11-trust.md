**Goal:** Settings, the app lock, hide amounts, encrypted backup and restore through the system file picker with a weekly automatic backup, export to Excel, the notifications (daily reminder and the few useful ones), the home-screen widgets, Arabic/English switching, and the privacy, terms and licences screens.

## Decisions taken in this plan (owner may override)

1. **One PIN.** The app lock reuses Phase 9b's `PinStore` (the child-screen PIN is the same parent PIN). Turning the lock on asks for a PIN if none is set. Locks on cold start and after 60 s in the background; while locked the app is hidden from recents (`FLAG_SECURE` on the window while the lock is on).
2. **Forgotten PIN** → one sentence explaining the only way in, then "erase this phone's data" (`DataWipe`) and the restore flow. No other bypass.
3. **Hide amounts** is a global setting read through a CompositionLocal by the shared money composables (Odometer, the amount text helpers, the ticket); every amount becomes dots. Screens that format amounts by hand are moved onto the shared helper where practical; the report lists what is covered.
4. **Backup file** = one file: a small header (magic, version, salt, iterations, created time, ticket count) + AES-256-GCM ciphertext of a zip holding a consistent copy of the Room database (checkpointed) and the settings DataStore file. Key from PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with a high iteration count. The password is never stored; the automatic backup keeps a Keystore-sealed copy of the derived key only (needed to write weekly without asking), and the screen says so plainly. Restore checks the header, names the backup's date and ticket count, asks for the password, then replaces everything and restarts the app (Task 44's relaunch).
5. **Where it is saved:** "اعمل نسخة دلوقتي" uses the system "create document" picker; the automatic backup asks once for a folder (`OPEN_DOCUMENT_TREE`, persisted permission) and keeps the last 4 files there. WorkManager weekly, at night, while charging; the failed-run line "آخر نسخة فشلت. جرّب تاني." on the backup screen and in Settings, never a full-screen alert.
6. **WorkManager** gets its Hilt `Configuration.Provider` now (closes the carry-forward item), and the midnight print worker is added (prints the new day's ticket in the background so widgets and notifications have it).
7. **Notifications:** the evening reminder at the user's chosen hour (only if the ticket is still open), and only these others, each with a switch in Settings: bill tomorrow, gameya installment tomorrow, gameya turn, 48 hours passed, occasion soon, streak at risk (once, 1 h before midnight), trial ending (the day before), subscription trial ending (the day before, from Phase 6's "فكّرني"), low balance (Phase 5's switch). Never more than two a day (a daily counter; priority order fixed in code). With the lock on, every amount in a notification is dots. The morning notification is off by default.
8. **Widgets** (Glance, already a dependency): small ticket (today's number and holes), small match, and wide (ticket + one-tap repeat chips). They read the stored day summary (never calculate); a one-tap logs the same repeat entry as Home and refreshes the widget. With the lock on, dots.
9. **Language** switch in Settings uses `AppCompatDelegate.setApplicationLocales` as onboarding does; Settings shows the current value.
10. **Feedback** opens an email draft to one support address constant (`SUPPORT_EMAIL`), left empty until the owner gives one; while empty the row is hidden. **Ticket sounds** (off by default) play the system click sound on a punch, a tear and a close — no new sound assets.
11. **Legal:** in-app privacy policy and terms screens (Arabic and English) rewritten for v2 — everything stays on the phone, no account, no network, backups are the user's own files, the SMS paste is read on the device and never stored — plus a licences screen for the bundled fonts (OFL). `docs/legal/*` rewritten to match. Play Billing and store text come in Phase 13.
