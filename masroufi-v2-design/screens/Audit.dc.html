MASROUFI · DESIGN AUDIT

Why it feels basic, and what fixes it

Reviewed from 13 screenshots of the current build: Home, Activity, Budgets, New budget, New goal, More and the add menu. Findings are ranked by how much they hold back clarity and the daily habit.

Money clarity 5/10

Critical 4

Purple soup: nothing leads the eye

Every surface is the same glowing purple glass with the same radius and border. The streak card outranks the money; grey text sinks into the background.

Fix · Near-black ink surfaces, one accent per meaning, glass only on things that float.

The hero answers the wrong question

«معاك كام» is a number you already know. It doesn't change your next decision.

Fix · Lead with «تقدر تصرف النهارده», a daily safe-to-spend that moves every time you log.

Gamification is a sticker, not a loop

A streak of 1 and a level bar with no points, no reward after logging, and no reason to come back tomorrow.

Fix · Log → reward screen (+points, streak grows, one good fact) → week dots → next badge.

The "smart" note isn't smart

«صرفت 1,105 لحد دلوقتي» repeats the number printed right above it.

Fix · Insights must say something new (pace, bill share, renewals) and offer one action.

Keep: this is your edge

  • Egyptian dialect copy that sounds like a person
  • Local goals: رمضان والعيد، الجواز، الجمعيات
  • RTL handled correctly, consistent Western digits
  • Privacy and offline-first as the promise
Major 7

Three doors to the same room

Expense, income and transfer appear as big tiles, as quick chips and in the + menu, taking a third of the first screen.

Fix · One center + button, quick chips with last amount, and type-to-log.

Low-contrast secondary text

Lavender on purple for dates, wallets and placeholders lands around 3:1.

Fix · Neutral greys tuned to at least 4.5:1 on every surface.

Money formatting is inconsistent

A trailing dot on «ج.م.», a truncated «1,105.00 ج.», and .00 on every amount.

Fix · «1,105 ج.م»: decimals only when non-zero, currency smaller and lighter.

The donut chart misleads

A double ring with a misaligned track, no percentages, and legend numbers without currency.

Fix · One stacked bar plus ranked rows with percentages.

Lists hide the useful part

Titles repeat the category, there are no daily totals, dates are absolute, and the Spotify row truncates.

Fix · Note or merchant as title, daily totals, «امبارح / النهارده».

Forms ask before they help

A currency field on every form in an EGP app, a preview that reads 0.00, and a $ icon on goals.

Fix · Amount first, live translation («يعني 40 في اليوم»), currency in settings.

Navigation has duplicates and dead ends

Five tabs plus a floating +, a Budgets empty state that is a wall, and two subscription entries with the same icon.

Fix · Four tabs and a center +; Budgets and Goals merge into «الخطة».

Polish 6

Content runs under the status bar

The insights header collides with the clock when you scroll.

Fix · A sticky header with a solid scrim.

The + menu leaks

Blurred ghost text shows behind the pills, and they cover the list.

Fix · A bottom sheet with a type switch and keypad.

Picker overload

12 colours with a ragged second row, plus an icon grid that repeats the templates.

Fix · The template sets icon and colour; 6 swatches to override.

Fixed buttons clip fields

Save floats over the target-amount field with no separation.

Fix · A solid footer bar with a hairline border.

Oversized greeting

A huge «مساء الخير» takes the most valuable pixels on the screen.

Fix · A small greeting; the space goes to the daily number.

No backup nudge

An offline-first app with no backup is one lost phone away from zero.

Fix · A persistent card in More until backup is on.

Addictive, but honest

  • Reward logging, never spending
  • Streak freeze: one missed day doesn't erase a month
  • No guilt copy; every warning comes with one fix
  • One daily number beats ten notifications
