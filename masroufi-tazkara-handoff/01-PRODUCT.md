# Product

## Who it is for (first wins any trade-off)

1. **Salaried employees** paid once a month who need the salary to last: rent, bills, transport, groceries.
2. **Household managers** running the home budget: groceries, school costs, gameya, family occasions.
3. **Students and young adults** on an allowance or a first income, mostly cash and mobile wallets.
4. **Freelancers and daily earners** with irregular income who need to know what is really left.

## The promise

"كل يوم ليك تذكرة": every day you know one number, what you can spend today, and following it is enough for your
money to reach payday. Logging a spend takes two taps.

## Why people come back (the loop)

1. **Morning:** a fresh ticket with today's number. (Curiosity: how much do I get today?)
2. **During the day:** log a spend, punch a hole, watch the number drop. (Instant, physical feedback.)
3. **Night:** tear the ticket off. Leftover drops in the jar. A proverb card flips over. (Completion + a small,
   varied reward.)
4. **Week:** the weekly match, you against last week. (Friendly competition with yourself.)
5. **Month and year:** the jar grows, shown in grams of gold; seasons (Ramadan, Eid, school) get funded.

This is habit-forming on purpose, and honest on purpose: every reward is tied to a real improvement in the user's
money. No fake scarcity, no loot boxes, no shaming.

## Principles

1. **One number.** The ticket is the home screen. Everything else is one tap away, never in front of it.
2. **Two taps to log.** Repeated spends are one tap.
3. **Payday is the clock.** Days until payday matter more than calendar months.
4. **Money as Egyptians talk about it.** الكاش، المحفظة، تحت البلاطة، النوتة، الجمعية، النقطة، الفكة.
5. **Calm, never judging.** Over the ticket today? "اتوزّعوا على باقي الأيام". No red alarms, no guilt.
6. **Private by default.** No account. Nothing leaves the phone without an explicit action.
7. **Honest numbers.** No invented statistics, reviews or comparisons with other users. The only competitor is
   last week's you.
8. **The hardest month gets the most help.** End-of-month mode is free forever.

## Voice

Qersh talks like a sensible, funny friend from the neighbourhood. Short sentences. Egyptian colloquial. Warm, a
little cheeky, never preachy. Security, backup, payment and legal text stays plain and precise. Full rules and
the word list are in `07-COPY.md`.

## What it is not

- Not a bank, a wallet or a payment app. It records; it never moves money. Provider names (Vodafone Cash,
  InstaPay, Fawry, bank names) appear as plain text labels only.
- Not financial advice. It shows the user's own numbers and simple arithmetic.
- Not a social network. No public profiles or leaderboards. Sharing is a picture the user chooses to send.
